Le La El La: French & Spanish

Noun gender trainer for iPhone and iPad

Is it le or la? El or la? Gender is the thing French and Spanish learners never quite stop getting wrong. Le La El La trains it: you see a noun, pick masculine or feminine, and it shows you the ending rule that applies, and the exceptions to it.

Nouns you get wrong, or answer slowly, come up more often. Your progress is broken down by ending, so you can see whether -tion, -eur or -ción is the problem. Race the clock in the Arcade when you're ready.

The 500 commonest nouns of each language are free. A one-time purchase unlocks every noun (about 19,000 French and 9,000 Spanish), the higher Arcade levels and the Exceptions drill. No account, no subscription and no ads, and it works offline.
